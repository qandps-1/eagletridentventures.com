Our Partners

Leadership driving strategic investments in minerals, defense tech, and energy & infrastructure.

Bob Smith

Partner | Principal & CEO, Adobe Capital Partners and Infrastructure X

A 30-year energy, infrastructure, and capital markets executive, Bob brings deep expertise in private equity, venture capital, deal structuring, and complex transactions. He previously served as EVP, General Counsel, and Chief Development Officer of Pinnacle West Capital (NYSE: PNW), and led Columbia Pipeline Group through a $13 billion sale following its double IPO spin-off from NiSource.

As President of El Dorado Investment Corporation, PNW's venture capital vehicle, Bob oversaw more than $100 million in investment assets across energy technology, defense tech, advanced computing, AI, fintech, and critical minerals. He currently advises several technology funds including Energy Impact Partners, The Westly Group, AZ VC Fund, and Asterozoa Capital Partners.

Bob's network spans C-suite officers at the largest energy companies in the US and Canada and senior bankers at leading global investment banks. He holds a law degree from The Ohio State University and a bachelor's degree from the University of South Alabama.

Beyond business, Bob chairs the board of Global Action, an international compassion and leader training organization, and is founder of the GoSat Initiative, a multi-ministry effort to embed core Christian principles in geopolitically changing nations.

Bruce Roberts

Partner | CEO & Founder, Trident Solutions

A retired U.S. Army Lieutenant Colonel with nearly three decades of operational experience spanning logistics, intelligence, infantry, and public relations, Bruce brings rare cross-domain expertise to defense and infrastructure engagements. His final assignments included service as a Special Operations Battalion Commander and Division Assistant Operations Officer.

After retiring in 2021, Bruce founded August Mission, a nonprofit that supported U.S. citizens and Afghan allies during the U.S. withdrawal from Afghanistan. As CEO of Trident Solutions, he provides strategic leadership and cross-domain coordination, guiding complex engagements with clarity and operational precision across defense, dual-use technology, and critical infrastructure sectors.

Nicholas Fowler

Partner | President & Founder, Trident Solutions

An operational leader with more than 20 years of experience across defense, aviation, and humanitarian operations in high-risk and politically sensitive environments, Nicholas has built and led partnerships across government, NGOs, and private ventures in austere conditions worldwide.

As President of Trident Solutions, Nicholas leads international strategy, coordinates high-value introductions, and manages senior-level engagements with defense and government stakeholders across Europe and beyond. His operational depth and global network make him a trusted bridge between strategic vision and on-the-ground execution.

John Loudon

Partner | Former State Senator

A former state senator, serial entrepreneur, and international business leader, John brings over 30 years of experience across startups, policy, and strategic investment — with a powerful network spanning government, defense, energy, and media at the highest levels.

In the Senate, John founded and chaired the Small Business Committee for eight years, authoring landmark rewrites of workers' compensation, unemployment, and health insurance law.

John and his wife, Dr. Gina Loudon — media personality and President Trump media advisor — were Trump delegates in 2016 and Mar-a-Lago members with deep access across the minerals, energy, and defense sectors. His current holdings include a hydrogen energy company, a global broadcast studio business serving national and international news networks, and a consumer products franchise operation.

Antonio Misuraca

Partner

George Sampas

Partner | M&A Partner, Gibson Dunn

George is a partner in Gibson Dunn's New York office, Co-Chair of its Mergers & Acquisitions Practice Group and Head of Cross-Border M&A. His practice focuses on cross-border transactions, unsolicited acquisitions and private equity transactions, with an emphasis on aviation, aerospace and defense, and energy and minerals.

George received his law degree from The University of Chicago Law School and his Bachelor of Arts from Brown University, and is admitted to practice in the State of New York. He is regularly ranked in publications such as New York Super Lawyers, The Legal 500 United States, Euromoney's Guide to the World's Leading Mergers and Acquisitions Lawyers and Who's Who Legal, and was named by Global Counsel as one of its "Highly Recommended M&A Practitioners."

George represents leading global corporations on their largest, most complex and transformative deals, including Alcan, Anheuser-Busch InBev, Aventis, BBA Aviation, BCE, Columbia Pipeline Group, Enbridge, Inco, KPMG, Mitsubishi Tokyo Financial Group and Vodafone Group, as well as private equity firms and investment advisors.

Historic transactions include representing Vodafone in its acquisition of Mannesmann, the largest M&A transaction in history; Elf in its defense and merger with Total; Alcan in its unsolicited acquisition of Pechiney and its sale to Rio Tinto; InBev in its unsolicited acquisition of Anheuser-Busch and AB InBev's later acquisition of Modelo; and Inco in many transactions, including its sale to Vale.

Anna Sampas

Partner

A Harvard Law graduate, Anna has spent much of her career in intellectual property licensing, leading the IP transactions practice at Sullivan & Cromwell and serving as lead technology lawyer for NBC Universal. She also brings deep entrepreneurial experience in real estate and technology services.

At Sullivan & Cromwell, Anna worked closely with MPEG LA, the first patent pool licensing company, and Thomson Licensing, and represented SK Hynix in semiconductor licensing projects, including its first relationship with TSMC. From 2007 to 2012 she was lead technology lawyer for NBC Universal, negotiating software, development, service and hardware agreements and managing the legal aspects of adding emerging technologies to the business.

Anna developed a real estate business that purchased, renovated and rented residential and vacation properties, and early in her career was general counsel of an IT services company. She is bringing together her entrepreneurial experience and community service, which has included serving on the Board of the Brooklyn Academy of Music, to found and grow Dåna Pada, a charity that sends trained therapists, teachers and doctors to assist children with special needs in developing countries.
